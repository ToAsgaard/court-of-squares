Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what information Court of Squares ("the game") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The game is made by Steven Elkins ("we", "us"), Kentucky, United States, who is responsible for your information under this policy. You can reach us at courtofsquares@gmail.com.

1. THE SHORT VERSION

  • You can play without giving us your name, email or any contact details. A guest account is created automatically.
  • We collect what the game needs to work: your account, your progress and settings, and what other players can see when you play online.
  • We don't show ads, we don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use advertising or cross-app tracking. Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports are only sent if you agree, and you can turn them off any time.
  • There is no free-text chat. Players communicate only through preset emotes.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time from Settings > Account > Delete account.

2. WHAT WE COLLECT

Account information

  • A player ID created by Unity Authentication when you first open the game (a random identifier, not your name).
  • The player name you choose. Names are checked by an automatic filter for offensive words.
  • If you choose to create a login: the username you pick and your password (the password is stored by Unity Authentication in protected form; we never see it).
  • If you choose to link an account: the identifier that Steam, Apple (Sign in with Apple) or Google Play Games gives us for that account. We don't receive your password for those services. If you use Sign in with Apple, we do not request your name or email. We do not read your Steam name, friends or profile.

Game progress and settings

  • Your progress: statistics, piece mastery, unlocks, Garden layout and puzzle record, Pilgrimage runs, saved armies, cosmetic choices (table, pieces, music, crest and banner), settings and preferences.
  • A copy of this progress is saved to the cloud (cloud save) so you can restore it on another device. It includes the date of the save and the name of the device that saved it (the name set on the device, or its model), so you can choose between two copies.
  • Saved games against the computer, replays, and games where you watch the computer play stay on your device and are not uploaded.

What other players can see (when you play online)

  • Your player name, profile card (crest, banner, rank title, and a mark if you own the Court Pass), online rank and rating, piece mastery, trophies, and your Garden (layout and flower count).
  • The piece set you play with, so opponents can see your pieces in your chosen set (unless they choose to see every piece in their own set).
  • Your statistics, unless you set them to Private in Settings > Account. Choosing Private also keeps your Garden private.
  • While your game room is open in the Open Games list: your player name and rating.
  • Leaderboard entries (Garden daily puzzle, Weekly Challenge, Pilgrimage) with your name and result.
  • Head-to-head records between you and the players you have played.

Online play and social features

  • The moves, settings and results of online games (including Draft picks and the armies you place), and saved online games (kept for up to 30 days).
  • Your rating, used to match you with players of similar skill.
  • Your friends list, friend code, friend requests, game invitations, the players you have blocked, and whether you are online or in a game.
  • Preset emotes you send during games (these are delivered live and not stored).
  • Flowers you leave in other players' Gardens (one per Garden per day).
  • Reports you make about other players, and reports made about you, so we can look into problems.

Purchases

  • Purchases are made through Apple, Google or Steam. They handle your payment details; we never receive your card or bank information. We receive a confirmation of the purchase (such as a transaction or receipt identifier) and send it to Apple or Google to check that it is genuine. We keep a record that your account owns the Court Pass (which store and when), so we can unlock it on your account and restore it on your other devices.

Usage statistics and crash reports (only if you agree)

  • When you first open the game it asks whether it may send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. You can change your answer any time in Settings > About > Usage data.
  • If you agree, the game sends events such as which modes are played, game results, Pilgrimage stages reached, Garden puzzles solved and whether the Court Pass screen was opened or the pass unlocked, together with Unity's random installation identifier, the device model, operating system, language and game version. Crash and error reports include the technical details of the problem.
  • These go to Unity Technologies (Unity Analytics and Unity Cloud Diagnostics). They don't include your player name, friends or anything you type. If you turn sharing off, the game also asks Unity to delete the usage data already collected from your installation.

Technical information

  • Like any online service, the servers that run the game (Unity Gaming Services) process your device's IP address and basic technical information (such as device type, operating system and game version) to connect you, keep the service secure and fix problems. To prevent abuse, the servers briefly count how often each player ID makes requests; these counts are kept only in memory and are not stored.
  • The Unity engine may also send basic crash and performance diagnostics to Unity.

Notifications (phones, only if you allow them)

  • Daily puzzle reminders are scheduled on your phone itself; nothing is sent to us for them.
  • If you allow notifications for friend requests and game invites, your phone's push token (an identifier from Apple or Google that lets a notification reach that phone), the game's language on it and your notification choices are stored with your account, so we can tell you when a friend writes while you're away.

Sharing images

  • If you share an image the game makes (for example a game result or your Garden), it is sent through your device's own share options to the app or person you choose. We don't receive a copy.

Support emails

  • If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, and use it only to answer you.

3. HOW WE USE IT

We use this information only to:

  • run your account, save your progress and restore it on your devices;
  • run online games, matchmaking, ratings, friends, leaderboards, Gardens and other features you use;
  • unlock and restore purchases;
  • keep the game fair and safe (preventing cheating and abuse, enforcing blocks, filtering names);
  • review reports and enforce the rules (see "Moderation" below);
  • understand how the game is played and fix crashes, if you allow usage statistics and crash reports;
  • answer your support requests; and
  • fix bugs and improve the game.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Legal bases (for players in the EEA, UK and similar regions): we process your information to provide the game you asked for (performance of a contract); for our legitimate interests in keeping the game secure, fair and working, and in reviewing reports; with your consent for usage statistics and crash reports; and to comply with legal obligations. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

Moderation

  • When players report someone, we keep the report (who reported whom, the reason, the game it came from and when) so we can review it.
  • A player name reported as offensive by several different players is replaced automatically with a plain one ("Player 1234"). We may also replace names, or suspend an account from online features for a time, when the rules in our Terms of Service are broken. We keep a record of suspensions and replaced names.

4. WHO WE SHARE IT WITH

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, Cloud Code and Cloud Save; and, only if you agree, Unity Analytics and Cloud Diagnostics) hosts the game's online services and stores account and game data on our behalf. See Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Apple, Google and Valve (Steam), when you sign in with, link, or buy through their services. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and Apple (Apple Push Notification service), which deliver notifications to your phone if you turn them on.
  • Google (Gmail), which hosts our support email.
  • Other players see the information described in "What other players can see".
  • Authorities, if we are required to by law, or to protect the safety of players or others.
  • A buyer or successor, if the game is sold or transferred, under the same protections described here.

We do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are used in California law.

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where Unity's services operate. Where required, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

5. HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

We keep your account and game data until you delete your account. Saved online games expire after 30 days. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you.

When you delete your account:

  • your account, cloud save, public profile, rating, friends, friend requests, blocks, flowers, Pilgrimage data, presence, notification tokens, purchase record and saved online games are deleted;
  • your head-to-head records are deleted from your side; other players keep their own records, which then show "Former player";
  • your leaderboard entries are kept but anonymized (shown as "Former player");
  • records of finished online games (which contain no contact details), reports about player conduct, and suspension records may be kept for security and moderation; and
  • everything the game stored on that device is erased.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a purchase. The store you bought the Court Pass from keeps its own record, so you can unlock it again on a new account: on iPhone and iPad with Restore purchases, on Android automatically when the game starts (signed in to the same Google account), and on Steam automatically, because the game includes the pass.

Usage statistics already sent to Unity Analytics are deleted when you turn sharing off in Settings > About > Usage data.

6. YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

  • Delete your account and data: Settings > Account > Delete account (an internet connection is needed). You can also request it without the game, on the account deletion page of our website or by emailing courtofsquares@gmail.com, and we will do it for you.
  • Make your statistics and Garden private: Settings > Account > My statistics.
  • Stop usage statistics and crash reports: Settings > About > Usage data.
  • See opponents' pieces only in your own set: Settings > Look > Opponent's pieces.
  • Turn notifications on or off: Settings > Device > Notifications, or your phone's settings.
  • Block players: from their profile card.
  • Access, correct or receive a copy of your information, or object to or restrict how we use it: email us. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, California or other US states), you have these rights by law. We will not treat you differently for using them.
  • When you email us, we may ask you to confirm the account is yours (for example by sending your friend code from inside the game) before we act. We answer within one month.
  • You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. CHILDREN

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or under the age required by law in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. SECURITY

Data is sent to Unity's services over encrypted connections and stored by them with industry-standard protections. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

9. CHANGES

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the game.
