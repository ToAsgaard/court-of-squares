The Pilgrimage

A roguelite journey with no end. Lead a small band of pilgrims from battle to battle against armies that keep growing and keep getting smarter. Every piece you lose stays lost, so every capture you allow matters for the rest of the road.

  1. Fight

    Win within 50 moves against the stage's army. There are no draws: if the moves run out, the side with more points on the board wins, and a tie goes to the enemy.

  2. Make camp

    Wins pay tithes. Spend them on recruits (five on offer, one at half price), pay a scout to see exactly where the next enemy stands, and rearrange your army before you march on.

  3. Choose a relic

    Every third stage, pick one of three relics. Each one bends the rules in your favour for the rest of the journey.

Eight relics

Pick well. They stack, and no two journeys offer them in the same order.

Boots of the Long March15 more moves in every battle.

Who you'll meet

Themed companies like the Bowmen of the Vale, the Jester Troupe and the Cloister Guard. Every fifth stage, a boss court such as the Hollow Court or the Ashen King's Guard brings a bigger army.

Your way

Start with a king, bishop, knight, rook and four pawns. Play Muster with every piece in the game, or Classic with the standard six, on Easy, Normal or Hard.

Climb the leaderboard

Every version and difficulty has its own board, ranked by the most stages cleared. Ranked journeys are checked by the server battle by battle, so every score is real.

Ways to play

Play the classic game, or bring the new pieces in. The clock is off unless you want one, and any game can add rule twists like King of the Hill.

Calm by design

  • No ads, ever. The game is free. The optional Court Pass is one purchase that opens the full Pilgrimage, garden decorating and every cosmetic you earn.
  • No clock unless you want one, or anything from 1-minute bullet to 30-minute classical.
  • Press and hold any piece to see exactly how it moves.
  • Close-up or top-down board, with coordinates, legal moves and move trails on or off.
  • Comfortable on any device: adjustable text size, pinch to zoom, and lighter effects for older phones and longer battery life.
  • Twelve languages: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Polish, Turkish, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.
