Calm chess with eight unique pieces. Help test it on Android before launch.

Join the Android beta

It's free and takes about a minute. Use the same Google account that your Android phone's Play Store uses for all three steps.

  1. Join the tester group

    Sign in with your Google account and click Join group. You won't get emails from it.

    Join the tester group

  2. Become a tester

    Open this link and tap Become a tester. If it says the app isn't available yet, wait a few minutes after step 1 and open it again.

    Become a tester

  3. Download from Google Play

    Install Court of Squares from Google Play on your Android phone or tablet.

    Get it on Google Play

How you can help

  • Keep the beta for at least two weeks. Google only lets a new game launch after its testers have been in the beta for 14 days, so staying in really helps.
  • Play a few times: a game against the computer, the Daily Puzzle and the Garden, and an online game if you can.
  • Tell us what feels confusing, slow or broken. Leave private feedback on the game's Google Play page, post in our Discord, or email courtofsquares@gmail.com.

Good to know

  • Your progress carries over to the full release. Link an account in Settings › Account to keep it safe across devices.
  • The game is free with no ads. The optional Court Pass is a real one-time purchase, even in the beta, and it stays yours after launch.
  • You can leave the beta any time from the "Become a tester" page.

Trouble joining? Make sure you're signed in to the same Google account on this page and in the Play Store. If the Play Store still doesn't show the game, email us and we'll sort it out.
