About the game

Short description

Court of Squares is a calm, painterly chess game with eight new pieces, armies you build yourself, an endless roguelite Pilgrimage and a garden that grows with every daily puzzle.

Longer description

Every rule you know, on ornate tables with soft music and pieces that glide. Then eight new pieces join the board: an Archer that shoots from two squares away, a Jester that captures by chain-jumping, a Nightrider that leaps again and again, and the Lancer, Herald, Falconer, Abbess and Pilgrim.

Build your own army on a points budget, draft pieces against an opponent, or take a small band of pilgrims on the Pilgrimage: an endless roguelite journey where lost pieces stay lost, wins pay tithes for new recruits, relics bend the rules and a boss court waits every fifth stage. A new puzzle every day grows a garden you design, which changes with the seasons.

Key features

  • Eight new pieces, each with its own move and lesson
  • Classic chess, Muster, Draft and Pawnstorm, against the computer or a friend
  • The Pilgrimage: an endless roguelite with relics, tithes and boss courts
  • A daily puzzle and a garden that changes through the seasons
  • A Weekly Challenge leaderboard
  • Online play with friends and a server-checked ranked ladder
  • Piece sets, tables, music and seasonal events to make it your own
  • Replays, saved armies, share pictures and clips, and a relaxing Watch mode
  • One account across phone, tablet and PC
