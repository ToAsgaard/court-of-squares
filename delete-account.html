Delete Your Account

This page explains how to delete your Court of Squares account and its data. The game is made by Steven Elkins.

In the game (fastest)

Open Settings › Account › Delete account and confirm. It needs an internet connection and takes a few seconds. Your account and data are deleted right away, and the game starts over as a new guest.

Without the game

If you no longer have the game installed, email us and we'll delete it for you:

Email a deletion request

Or write to courtofsquares@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request" and include:

  • your player name;
  • your friend code, if you know it (it's on the Social tab in the game);
  • how you signed in (guest, a username, Steam, Apple or Google Play Games), and the username if you made one.

We may ask a question to make sure the account is yours. We delete the account within 30 days of confirming it, usually much sooner, and email you when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in, cloud save, public profile, rating, friends list and friend requests, blocks, flowers, Pilgrimage data, online status, Court Pass record and saved online games.
  • Your head-to-head records (other players keep their own records, which then show "Former player").
  • Your leaderboard entries are kept but anonymized (shown as "Former player").

What may be kept

  • Records of finished online games (which contain no contact details), reports about player conduct, and suspension records, for security and moderation.
  • Anonymous usage statistics are not tied to your account. They are deleted when you turn off Settings › About › Usage data in the game.

Purchases

Deleting your account doesn't refund or cancel a purchase. The store keeps its own record, so you can unlock the Court Pass again on a new account: with Restore purchases on iPhone and iPad, automatically on Android with the same Google account, and automatically on Steam.
